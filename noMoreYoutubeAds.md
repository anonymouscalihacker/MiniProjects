
# 📖 Guide: Avoid Ads Using The Native Yotube App/Website

YouTube ads can be blocked by changing your account's location to a **non-monetized country** where YouTube does not serve ads. Follow this step-by-step guide to configure it on both desktop and mobile devices.

---

## ✅ **Step 1: Understand Non-Monetized Countries**
YouTube serves ads in specific "monetized" markets. To avoid ads, you must choose a location that is **NOT monetized**. 

### Subset: Some Country Restrictions
Some non-monetized countries may **not allow access to all videos** due to:
- **Geopolitical restrictions**
- **Licensing issues**
- **Local regulations** restricting specific content

To ensure access to all desired videos, you may need to **switch between non-monetized countries**.

### Full List of Non-Monetized Countries Available in Youtube Settings List:
- **Belarus**
- **Libya**
- **Kazaksthan**
- **Nepal**
- **Laos**



---

## ✅ **Step 2: Change Location on Desktop**
1. **Open YouTube**:
   - Go to [https://www.youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com) and log in to your account.

2. **Access Account Settings**:
   - Click on your **profile icon** at the top-right corner of the screen.
   - In the dropdown menu, locate and click on **Location**.
     <br><img width="297" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/37b52c9e-5ef5-4b52-9e4e-ca2a8c18cb93" />


3. **Select a Non-Monetized Country**:
   - From the list, scroll and select a non-monetized country such as:
     - **Belarus**
     - **Nepal**
     - **Kazakhstan**
       <br><img width="296" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/09d6d215-7983-4d6f-8a9c-98aa4837ceea" />

4. **Refresh YouTube**:
   - Reload the YouTube page to apply the changes.

---

## ✅ **Step 3: Change Location on Mobile (YouTube App)**
1. **Open YouTube App**:
   - Launch the YouTube app on your mobile device.

2. **Go to Account Settings**:
   - Tap your **Profile Icon** (top-right corner).
   - Select **Settings** > **General**.

3. **Change Location**:
   - Locate the **Location** option.
   - From the dropdown, select a non-monetized country such as:
     - **Libya**
     - **Nepal**
     - **Kazakhstan**

4. **Restart the App**:
   - Fully close and reopen the YouTube app to apply the new location.

---

## ✅ **Step 4: Verify Location Change**
1. Go to the YouTube homepage and play a few videos.
2. Confirm that ads are no longer appearing.

---

## Optional: Use a VPN (If Countries Are Unavailable)
If your desired non-monetized country isn't listed:
1. Install and configure a **VPN** application (e.g., ProtonVPN, NordVPN).
2. Connect to a server located in one of the non-monetized countries (e.g., **Nepal** or **Libya**).
3. Open YouTube and verify that ads are no longer showing.

---

## 📝 Notes
- This method works for both desktop and mobile YouTube apps.
- Changing your location only impacts **YouTube's ad targeting**. It does not change your **Google account location**.
- For a seamless experience, consider using a reliable VPN if the countries are unavailable in your settings.

---
